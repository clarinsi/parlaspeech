ParlaSpeech - Richly annotated spoken corpora of parliamentary debates

ParlaSpeech is a collection of spoken corpora of the Croatian, Czech, Polish, Serbian, and Slovenian parliaments. The corpora are built from the transcripts of parliamentary proceedings available in the ParlaMint 4.0 corpus and the parliamentary recordings available from the parliaments' YouTube channels. An instance is a transcript sentence with the corresponding metadata and the aligned audio.

Each sentence keeps the rich ParlaMint metadata on the speaker (name, gender, party, role) and the session (date, term, agenda), and is enriched with automatic annotations in both modalities: filled and silent pauses, word- and grapheme-level alignment and primary word stress on the speech side, and linguistic annotation, sentiment, and CAP topics on the text side. This makes ParlaSpeech useful across political discourse analysis, phonetics, corpus linguistics, and speech technology development.

5 parliaments 10k+ hours of speech 3.9M aligned sentences 7 enrichment layers CC BY-SA 4.0

NEW! ParlaSpeech 4.0 adds the Slovenian parliament, a new enrichment layer of silent pauses, and CAP topic labels as speech-level metadata. The release is available in the CLARIN.SI repository, and all five corpora can be searched in the CLARIN.SI concordancers.

Enrichment layers

Speech-side layers are inferred from the audio, text-side layers from the transcript. Word alignment and primary stress are currently available for Croatian and Serbian only.

    TEI XML, TXT, CoNLL-U · source of transcripts and metadata

Formats

The same data are distributed in four formats, each aimed at a different kind of use.

  • JSONL
    Master format · all parliaments · all layers
    Newline-delimited JSON, where each line is a valid JSON serialization of one sentence with all its metadata and enrichments. Mostly intended for computerized processing. See the dataset schema and example entry below.
  • FLAC
    Audio · all parliaments
    Per-sentence audio recordings, referenced from each JSONL entry through the audio field.
  • VERT
    Vertical format · for concordancers
    Text with links to audio, linguistic annotation, sentiment, filled pauses, and primary word stress (where available). This is the format behind the CLARIN.SI noSketch Engine concordancers.
    Concordancer guide
  • TextGrid
    Praat format · HR and RS only
    Word- and grapheme-level alignment, primary word stress, and filled pauses as tiers, for use with Praat in phonetics and other speech-focused research.

Dataset schema

The fields of one JSONL instance, grouped by the layer they belong to. An instance is a single transcript sentence with its metadata and aligned audio.

Timing information: absolute times are timestamps from the original full-length session audio, so instances don't start at 0.0 s. Relative times are normalized to each extracted sentence, always beginning at 0.0 s.

Two word-level alignments: words is the ASR-level alignment, available for all parliaments, while words_align is a finer Kaldi MFA-level alignment, available for HR and RS only.

ParlaSpeech (base) all parliaments
id [string] Unique sentence ID, referencing the utterance ID from ParlaMint 4.0, expanded with character offsets.
words [list of dicts] ASR word-level timing and character offset annotations.
  • time_s [float] Start time of the word in seconds, relative.
  • time_e [float] End time of the word in seconds, relative.
  • char_s [int] Start character index in the transcript, relative.
  • char_e [int] End character index in the transcript, relative.
audio [string] Relative path to the audio file in ParlaSpeech.
audio_length [float] Duration of the audio file in seconds.
text [string] Text from the official manual transcriptions corresponding to the audio.
text_start [int] Start character index of the relevant text segment, absolute.
text_end [int] End character index of the relevant text segment, absolute.
audio_start [float] Start time of the relevant audio segment, absolute.
audio_end [float] End time of the relevant audio segment, absolute.
speaker_info [dict] Metadata about the speaker and session.
  • Text_ID [string] Text document ID.
  • ID [string] Speech segment ID.
  • Title [string] Title of the debate/session.
  • Date [string] Date of the session.
  • Body [string] Parliamentary body or chamber.
  • Term [string] Parliamentary term.
  • Session [string] Session number.
  • Meeting [string] Meeting number.
  • Sitting [string] Sitting number.
  • Agenda [string] Agenda topic.
  • Subcorpus [string] Subcorpus name.
  • Lang [string] Language of the transcript.
  • Speaker_role [string] Role of the speaker (e.g. Regular, Minister).
  • Speaker_MP [string] Whether the speaker is a Member of Parliament.
  • Speaker_minister [string] Whether the speaker is a minister.
  • Speaker_party [string] Party abbreviation.
  • Speaker_party_name [string] Full name of the party.
  • Party_status [string] Government, opposition or coalition.
  • Party_orientation [string] Political orientation (e.g. left, right).
  • Speaker_ID [string] Unique speaker identifier, usually in the format "LastnameFirstname".
  • Speaker_name [string] Full name of the speaker in the format "Lastname, Firstname".
  • Speaker_gender [string] Gender of the speaker (M, F or "-").
  • Speaker_birth [string] Birth year of the speaker.
ParlaSpeech-FilledPause all parliaments
filled_pauses [list of dicts] Timestamped intervals of filled pauses in the audio.
  • time_s [float] Start time of the pause in seconds, relative.
  • time_e [float] End time of the pause in seconds, relative.
  • words_idx [int] Index of the word in words preceding the pause.

[] (empty list) means no filled pauses were detected, whereas None means that inference failed for that entry.

ParlaSpeech-SilentPause all parliaments · new in 4.0
silent_pauses [list of dicts] Timestamped intervals of silent pauses in the audio.
  • time_s [float] Start time of the pause in seconds, relative.
  • time_e [float] End time of the pause in seconds, relative.
  • words_idx [int] Index of the word in words preceding the pause.
ParlaSpeech-Align HR and RS only
words_align [list of dicts] Kaldi MFA word-level alignment data. Each dictionary corresponds to a word.
  • text [string] Word text.
  • char_s [int] Start character index, relative.
  • char_e [int] End character index, relative.
  • time_s [float] Start time of the word in seconds, relative.
  • time_e [float] End time of the word in seconds, relative.
  • words_idx [int] Index of the word in words. Null if the instance cannot be matched to any words instance.
chars_align [list of lists of dicts] Kaldi MFA character-level alignment data. Every dictionary corresponds to a character, while each list of dictionaries corresponds to a word.
  • text [string] Character(s).
  • time_s [float] Start time of the character(s), relative.
  • time_e [float] End time of the character(s), relative.
  • char_s [int] Start index in the full text, relative.
  • char_e [int] End index in the full text, relative.
ParlaSpeech-Stress HR and RS only
primary_stress [list of dicts] Primary stress annotations for multisyllabic words.
  • words_align_idx [int] Index of the word in words_align.
  • stress [int] Index of the syllable containing the primary stress.
  • nuclei [list of int] Character indices of syllable nuclei.
  • raw [list of float] Inferred and unprocessed timestamped intervals of primary stress, relative to the audio file.
ParlaSpeech-Ling all parliaments
linguistic_annotation [list of dicts] Text-based word-level linguistic annotation.
  • words_idx [int] Index of the corresponding word in words. Null for parts of the text that cannot be found in words (such as punctuation).
  • id [int] Token ID.
  • text [string] Word form.
  • lemma [string] Lemma of the word.
  • upos [string] Universal part of speech.
  • xpos [string] Language-specific part of speech.
  • feats [string] Morphological features.
  • head [int] Head of the current word (syntactic dependency relation).
  • deprel [string] Type of syntactic dependency relation.
  • misc [string] Additional information.
ParlaSpeech-Senti all parliaments
sentiment [dict] Text-based sentiment prediction from the ParlaSent model.
  • ParlaSent_logit [float] Sentence sentiment prediction as a continuous value, ranging from 0 (negative) to 6 (positive).
  • ParlaSent_3 [string] Sentence sentiment on 3 levels (Negative, Neutral, Positive).
  • ParlaSent_6 [string] Sentence sentiment on 6 levels (Negative, Mixed Negative, Neutral Negative, Neutral Positive, Mixed Positive, Positive).
ParlaSpeech-Topic all parliaments · new in 4.0
cap [dict] Topic prediction from the ParlaCAP topic classifier. The label is assigned at the level of the speech, so all sentences of one speech share it.
  • topic [string] Topic according to the Comparative Agendas Project schema. Where no topic is prevailing, the label is "Mix".
  • prob [float] Probability of the topic label.

Models

      

        Openly available models used to produce the ParlaSpeech enrichment layers, and models trained on ParlaSpeech data.

    Filled pause detector
    
          
    Wav2Vec2-BERT · audio frame classification · ParlaSpeech-FilledPause
    
          
    
            A speech transformer fine-tuned on Slovenian to detect filled pauses directly from the speech signal. It generalizes well to other South and West Slavic languages, which allows its use on all five parliaments.

    Primary stress identifier
    
          
    Wav2Vec2-BERT · audio frame classification · ParlaSpeech-Stress
    
          
    
            A speech transformer fine-tuned on a new Croatian dataset to identify the syllable carrying primary stress in multisyllabic words, with near-perfect results on Croatian and Serbian.

    ParlaSent model
    
          
    XLM-R · text regression · ParlaSpeech-Senti
    
          
    
            A multilingual transformer for sentiment analysis of parliamentary texts, fine-tuned on manually annotated sentences from seven parliaments. Its continuous output is mapped to 3- and 6-level sentiment labels.

History

      
How ParlaSpeech grew from one parliament to five.

      
    
        
  1. 
          2021 The idea of the ParlaSpeech corpora was born while proposing the second iteration of the ParlaMint project, inside which textual transcripts were made available for 20+ European parliaments. We ideated a task to deliver at least 50 hours of aligned text and speech for at least three parliaments / languages. ParlaSpeech v1 and v2 were born inside the ParlaMint project.
        
    2. 
        
  2. 
          2022 ParlaSpeech v1 aligned the Croatian parliament spoken data to the existing Croatian ParlaMint transcripts. The results are the ParlaCLARIN workshop paper describing the construction process, and the ParlaSpeech-HR v1 corpus, 1,816 hours in size.
        
    3. 
        
  3. 
          2023 ParlaSpeech v2 was the final result of the ParlaMint project, spanning the parliaments of Croatia, Czechia, Poland, and Serbia, and 5,000 hours of audio. The improved alignment process, applied to the Croatian, Polish and Serbian parliaments, is described in the SPECOM conference paper, while the Czech corpus was compiled from the previously constructed Czech parliamentary speech corpus.
        
    4. 
        
  4. 
          2025 ParlaSpeech v3 enriched the audio and transcripts of v2 with UD-based linguistic annotation, filled pauses and primary stress, more detailed word- and character-level alignment, and transcript-based sentiment. It was presented at LREC 2026.
        
    5. 
        
  5. 
          2026 ParlaSpeech v4 adds the Slovenian parliament, silent pauses as a new enrichment layer, and CAP topic labels as speech-level metadata, reaching more than 10,000 hours across five parliaments. Extensions to the Bulgarian, Bosnian and Ukrainian parliaments are planned.
